## What internet speed is enough for Sony TV IPTV?

A beautiful Sony panel cannot rescue a struggling Wi-Fi connection. The TV can display a great picture; it cannot negotiate with physics.

TiviLive currently recommends at least **16 Mbps for HD** and **25+ Mbps for 4K/UHD**. It also says a wired Ethernet connection is preferred for stability, although a strong Wi-Fi connection can work.

This is worth checking before blaming the IPTV player for buffering.

If HD works but 4K starts spinning its little loading wheel at the worst possible moment — usually during the part of the game you actually care about — the connection is one of the first things to test.

TiviLive's FAQ also recommends switching to Ethernet, restarting the router, closing other bandwidth-heavy applications, or reducing stream quality when buffering occurs.
